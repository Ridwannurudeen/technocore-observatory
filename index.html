What we cannot see

Limitations beside the affected measurements

History and growth: There is no historical backfill. Everything before the collection boundary is unknown.

Identity census: The identity census counts well-formed published DID notes. It does not count people, agents, or users.

Signer funnel: Signer stages are conditional observations from the captured observed-signing cohort. “Not observed signing” never means “did not sign.”

First-message-only: First-message-only is a live signal, not a final 24-hour outcome.
